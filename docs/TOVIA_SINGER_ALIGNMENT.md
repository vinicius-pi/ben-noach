# Tovia Singer alignment research

## Purpose

This document translates Rabbi Tovia Singer's public remarks about righteous gentiles, Noahide life, Tanakh, and readers emerging from Christianity into product principles. It is not a claim of endorsement and does not attribute halakhic rulings to him beyond what the cited sources support.

## Primary findings

### 1. The righteous gentile is not spiritually second-class

In his answer **“What is a Former Christian Minister To Do? Convert?”**, Rabbi Singer states that a non-Jew is not obligated to convert to Judaism, that non-Jews are required to observe the Seven Laws of Noah, and that a non-Jew who lives accordingly is regarded as a righteous gentile with a place in the World to Come. He explicitly rejects the idea that the numerical difference between seven Noahide categories and 613 Jewish commandments measures relative spiritual worth.

Source:
https://outreachjudaism.org/former-christian-minister-convert/

Product consequence:

- Treat Bnei Noach as a complete intended audience, not as prospective converts.
- Avoid copy implying that Jewish conversion is the “advanced” or “complete” version of the user's path.
- Build a deep product capable of supporting years of learning rather than a short conversion bridge.

### 2. Judaism has a responsibility to make Noahide information available

In the same answer, Singer says Judaism does not promote conversion but does believe the Jewish people have a duty to provide information to people interested in fulfilling the Noahide laws.

Product consequence:

- The project can present itself as access/infrastructure: making authentic sources intelligible and navigable.
- The product should make rabbinic and textual authority easier to reach, not substitute for it.
- “Learn where this comes from” is a core user promise.

### 3. The Internet unexpectedly brought gentiles and pastors into Jewish textual learning

In **“The Gentiles are Coming!”**, Singer writes that he did not anticipate how many gentiles would be affected by Outreach Judaism and describes laypeople and pastors leaving evangelical assumptions and embracing the One God of Israel.

Source:
https://outreachjudaism.org/the-gentiles-are-coming/

Product consequence:

- The target user is real and already arrives through the Internet.
- The product should assume sincere curiosity rather than polemical intent.
- A former Christian may arrive with substantial Bible familiarity but almost no knowledge of Jewish textual conventions. The interface should respect the former while supplying the latter.

### 4. The strongest method is textual: Hebrew, context, Jewish Scripture

Across Singer's Outreach Judaism material, the recurring method is to return to the Hebrew Scriptures, the literary context, and classical Jewish interpretation. His answers on Genesis 1, Isaiah 7, Isaiah 53, Psalm 22, and related missionary prooftexts repeatedly turn on language and context rather than branding.

Examples:
https://outreachjudaism.org/elohim-plural/
https://outreachjudaism.org/trinity-genesis/
https://outreachjudaism.org/alma-virgin/
https://outreachjudaism.org/gods-suffering-servant-isaiah-53/
https://outreachjudaism.org/crucifixion-psalm/

Product consequence:

- Hebrew should be first-class even for users who cannot yet read it.
- Context should be visible before comparative apologetics.
- A future “later interpretations” layer should be subordinate to the base Jewish reading.
- The product should make it easy to inspect surrounding chapters, lexical issues, speaker/subject, and classical commentary.

### 5. Keep controversy issue-focused rather than personally antagonistic

In a 2014 interview Singer explains that on air he wants to “stick to the issues” rather than make the dispute personal; he returns to Jewish Scripture and what it actually says.

Source:
https://jewlicious.com/2014/01/an-interview-with-anti-missionary-crusader-rabbi-tovia-singer/

Product consequence:

- The core interface is not an anti-Christian feed.
- Avoid humiliating or taunting language aimed at the reader's former religion.
- Let textual context do the disconfirming work.
- Comparative notes should be written as claims/readings and textual responses, not as attacks on people.

### 6. Noahide identity is a positive relationship with God and Israel's faith, not merely a list of prohibitions

Singer's published answer describes the righteous gentile as created in the image of God, walking a sacred path, and feeling a profound spiritual connection to the God of Israel and the Jewish people. A 2023 talk, preserved in a third-party transcript compilation that links the original video, goes even further in describing a Noahide as joining the faith of Israel without joining the nation of Israel. Because that wording is currently verified through a secondary transcript rather than an official transcript, it should be treated as a research lead, not a project quotation.

Primary/public source:
https://outreachjudaism.org/former-christian-minister-convert/

Secondary transcript lead:
https://radicaluniverse.org/uploads/files/Quotes-of-Rabbi-Tovia-Singer-Part-4-3-1.pdf
Original video cited by that transcript:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qMT7-WRmSos

Product consequence:

- Teach positive theological concepts: creation, image of God, providence, repentance, prayer, justice, charity, knowledge of God, and the universal future described by the prophets.
- Do not reduce the entire experience to seven prohibition cards.
- Keep Jewish covenantal distinctiveness clear without making the non-Jew feel extraneous to Tanakh's universal claims.

### 7. The messianic horizon is universal knowledge of God

Singer repeatedly emphasizes Isaiah 11:9, Zechariah 14:9, Zephaniah 3:9, and related passages as describing a future in which the nations know and serve the One God. A recent public talk transcript likewise centers the messianic age on worldwide knowledge of God rather than Jewish redemption in isolation.

Corroborating current transcript source:
https://frumtube.com/video/messianic-prophecies-are-unfolding-now-before-our-eyes-rabbi-tovia-singer--834a2ecf-6702-4ace-bb4f-c1b22c90f819

Product consequence:

- Nevi'im should eventually become a major product pillar, not an appendix after Torah.
- “The nations in the prophets” is a natural guided pathway.
- Isaiah should be read positively in its own structure before it becomes a comparative-Christianity module.

### 8. Singer is already embedded in Noahide educational networks

Singer has served on Noahide educational/advisory initiatives and has content distributed through Noahide networks. Noahide Academy currently lists him among its rabbis/founders/team ecosystem and has public collaborations built around people leaving Christianity and continuing into Torah/Noahide learning.

Sources:
https://www.noahideacademy.org/he/team
https://www.noahideacademy.org/post/rabbi-tovia-singer-and-rabbi-moshe-perets-establish-a-holy-bridge-to-help-you-going-out-of-x-tiani
https://www.noahidenations.com/index.php/about-us/rabbinic-advisory-board?showall=1

Product consequence:

- Sending him a Noahide-oriented reader is aligned with an audience he already encounters.
- The ask should be correction and usefulness, not endorsement-first marketing.
- A useful prototype should demonstrate the gap between deconstruction of Christian readings and constructive long-term Tanakh study.

## Reception evidence

### The audience problem exists

A 2017 Florida International University thesis on contemporary Noahidism used primary sources and a survey of self-identifying Noahides. Its central conclusion was that contemporary Noahide identity often functions as an end in itself rather than simply a gateway to Orthodox conversion.

Source:
https://digitalcommons.fiu.edu/record/11690

A 2023 report on Singer's Living L'Chaim appearance described large numbers of non-Jewish listeners and interest in Noahide life; the underlying interview is available from Living L'Chaim.

Sources:
https://www.livinglchaim.com/shows/inspiration-for-the-nation/one-jew-is-outsmarting-missionaries-at-their-own-game-rabbi-tovia-singer
https://torahtable.com/articles/parshas-shoftim-rabbi-tovia-singers-life-mission-to-rescue-jews-ensnared-by-christian-missionaries--bcd742ee-b72e-41e7-a896-f755bc597741

Noahide Academy created a 2025 course specifically for former Christians who had been influenced by teachers such as Tovia Singer and needed a next step into the Hebrew Scriptures.

Source:
https://www.noahideacademy.org/post/rediscovering-the-bible-from-a-jewish-perspective

This is close to direct validation of the product gap: after deconstruction, people need a constructive, source-based way to read Tanakh.

## Product personality likely to be well received

The strongest alignment is not “make an app that looks like Tovia Singer.” It is to embody the textual values that recur in his work:

- fearless confidence in Tanakh without theatrical aggression;
- Hebrew and literary context;
- explicit Jewish interpretive provenance;
- dignity for righteous gentiles;
- no pressure to convert;
- no imitation of Jewish covenantal obligations as a substitute religion;
- universal knowledge of Hashem as a positive horizon;
- transparent source checking;
- an easy bridge from beginner understanding to original sources.

## Proposed product promise

> **Read the Tanakh as a coherent Jewish text. Understand the classical voices that preserved its reading. Learn what is universal, what belongs to Israel's covenant, and where a Ben Noach stands — with every step traceable to its source.**

## Recommended first pathways

1. **Creation and Humanity** — Genesis 1–11.
2. **Who is a Ben Noach?** — source-based pathway from Genesis through Sanhedrin/Rambam and contemporary reviewed guidance.
3. **The One God** — Genesis, Deuteronomy, Isaiah, Psalms.
4. **The Nations in the Prophets** — Isaiah 2, 11, 40–66; Micah 4; Zephaniah 3; Zechariah 8 and 14.
5. **Reading Rashi for the First Time** — explain peshat, midrash, dibbur hamatchil, and why supercommentaries exist.
6. **Coming from a Christian Bible** — translation/context orientation without beginning from attack language.

## What to ask Rabbi Singer after the prototype exists

The best review request is concrete:

1. Does the product correctly dignify the righteous gentile path without implying conversion pressure?
2. Does the beginner commentary help a reader encounter Tanakh in Jewish context rather than merely replace one interpretive authority with another?
3. Are there passages or categories where he thinks the product would especially help former Christians after they leave New Testament theology?
4. Is the proposed separation between base Jewish reading and optional comparative readings sound?
5. Who should supervise the Noahide halakhic-scope layer?
6. Would he prefer the repository/project to be transferred to, or co-governed by, a rabbinic/institutional body if it becomes useful?

The project should be fully useful even if the answer to the final question is no.
