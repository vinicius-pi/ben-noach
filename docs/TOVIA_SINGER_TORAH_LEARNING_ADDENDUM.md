# Rabbi Tovia Singer — Noahide Torah-learning evidence addendum

## Why this addendum exists

The broader alignment memo establishes Singer's positive framing of righteous gentiles and his text-first method. This addendum addresses the narrower question: **is there evidence that Singer himself has taught Torah to an explicitly righteous-gentile/Noahide audience, rather than merely tolerating the idea in theory?**

## Direct practice evidence

A preserved 2009 podcast/course feed titled **“Torah Truths with Rabbi Tovia Singer”** was distributed through the **Noahide Nations Torah Network**. Episode descriptions identify Singer as the teacher, direct listeners to Noahide Nations for information on becoming a righteous gentile, and solicit support for continuing these Torah teachings.

The course introduction preserved in podcast transcripts states that the Torah course was provided free by Outreach Judaism through the Noahide Nations Torah Network and that Singer would guide listeners through Torah study.

Sources:

- Apple Podcasts — *Torah Truths with Rabbi Tovia Singer*: https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/torah-truths-with-rabbi-tovia-singer/id306762801
- Preserved transcript/feed metadata: https://podscan.fm/podcasts/torah-truths-with-rabbi-tovia-singer

### What this proves

It is strong evidence from **practice** that Singer participated in structured Torah teaching distributed to a Noahide/righteous-gentile network.

### What this does not prove

It is **not** a blanket halakhic ruling from Singer that every non-Jew may study every domain of Torah Oral law at any depth. The project's halakhic-scope policy still requires separate source analysis and rabbinic review.

This distinction is important: the evidence supports the positive product premise — that a righteous gentile can be a serious audience for Torah teaching — while leaving the exact study boundaries to qualified halakhic supervision.

## Product consequence

This removes a major unnecessary restraint from the product brief.

The application should not be designed as a tiny “Seven Laws FAQ” that avoids the Hebrew Bible out of fear that a gentile reader is an illegitimate Torah audience. Singer's own educational practice demonstrates a model in which righteous gentiles receive substantive Torah teaching.

The correct constraint is **scope and provenance**, not intellectual starvation.

Therefore the product should confidently build:

- a complete Tanakh reading architecture;
- beginner access to Hebrew and Jewish textual context;
- carefully selected classical commentary;
- guided explanation of Rashi;
- universal themes and prophetic passages relevant to the nations;
- deep links to full Jewish source libraries;
- a separately governed Noahide-halakhic layer.

## Strongest Singer-aligned product hypothesis

Singer's work often performs the first half of a journey:

**Christian prooftext / inherited theology → Hebrew text → context → Jewish reading → collapse of the prior claim.**

The missing productized second half is:

**Now what? → read Tanakh constructively → learn how Jewish commentary works → understand one's place as a righteous gentile → develop durable literacy → go deeper into primary sources.**

A 2025 Noahide Academy course explicitly targeted former Christians influenced by teachers such as Singer who wanted to rediscover the Hebrew Bible from a Jewish perspective, which independently validates this “aftercare” gap.

Source:
https://www.noahideacademy.org/post/rediscovering-the-bible-from-a-jewish-perspective

## Practical implication for outreach to Singer

When the prototype is ready, the pitch should not be:

> “I built an app that teaches non-Jews Torah. Please approve it.”

The stronger factual framing is:

> “Your work has helped many non-Jews leave Christian readings and recognize that they do not need to become Jews to serve Hashem. I built a source-first reader for the next step: helping that person read Tanakh constructively, understand Rashi and the classical Jewish context, and know when a question belongs to Noahide halakhic guidance. I would value correction of the prototype and its boundaries.”

This frames the product as an extension of an already observable educational problem rather than as a new religious institution seeking endorsement.
